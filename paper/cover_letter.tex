We submit the manuscript \emph{``An IoT-Enabled Hybrid Soft Sensor for Real-Time Residual-Moisture Monitoring and
Resource Accounting in Compact Wool Scouring Machines''} for consideration as a \textbf{Regular Paper} in the
IEEE Internet of Things Journal. We request \textbf{Traditional (non-open-access) publication}.

The paper addresses IoT trials/experiments and IoT application design, both within the scope of the journal. Its main
contributions are: (i) a low-cost ESP32-based IoT architecture for wool scouring machines, which today operate without
instrumentation; (ii) a hybrid soft sensor that uses a design-of-experiments model of the squeezing device as a
physics-informed prior and is recalibrated online from sparse laboratory samples; (iii) real-time per-kilogram energy and
water indicators and fault alarms; and (iv) a reproducible reanalysis of the historical mechanical-process baseline
and an open-source implementation (https://github.com/nasin0764-bit/woolwash-iot) with a defined prospective
field-validation protocol. \TODO{Update (iv) with the completed field validation before submission.}
