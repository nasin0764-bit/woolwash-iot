\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

We submit the manuscript \emph{``An IoT-Enabled Hybrid Soft Sensor for Real-Time Residual-Moisture Monitoring and
Resource Accounting in Compact Wool Scouring Machines''} for consideration as a \textbf{Regular Paper}. We request
\textbf{Traditional (non-open-access) publication}.

The work addresses IoT application design and IoT trials in an industrial setting that is currently not instrumented:
wool scouring in small regional plants. Its contributions are (i) a low-cost ESP32-based architecture with a captive
set-up portal, HTTP/MQTT transport, server analytics and a bilingual web/mobile application; (ii) a hybrid soft sensor
for residual moisture that uses a design-of-experiments model of the squeezing device as a prior, with a staged,
offset-shrinking online calibration from sparse laboratory samples; (iii) per-kilogram energy and water indicators and
alarm rules with debounce and hysteresis; and (iv) three clearly separated layers of evidence: a reproducible
reanalysis of historical mechanical-process data, a simulation and software bench study, and a factory validation
\TODO{describe the completed field campaign: N shifts at plant, held-out accuracy, delivery ratio, latency, kWh/kg,
L/kg}. The simulation reports openly where the prior helps (cold start, few labels, geometry changes) and where it
does not (many labels at a fixed geometry). Code, firmware and analysis scripts are public at
https://github.com/nasin0764-bit/woolwash-iot.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere.
